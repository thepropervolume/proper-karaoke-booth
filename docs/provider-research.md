# Provider and content metadata research

Initial public-source review, 2026-11-06. No playable provider is integrated. Descriptions are provider claims, not a contract or proof that a given use, territory or platform is supported. Confirm exact versions, coverage, access, price, terms, reporting and permitted caching/analysis directly before implementation.

Karaoke Nerds subsequently granted access to its community metadata feed and asked that it be called about once per day, with results cached. One successful initial fetch returned 64,261 records and seeded a local SQLite prototype; the central service and desktop catalog connection are not deployed. The snapshot contains YouTube watch links but no video files, brand prefixes, track codes or verified video publication dates. The `Created` field is not evidence of publication date. A metadata credential does not establish media-download rights. See [catalog groundwork](karaokenerds-catalog.md).

- [KaraFun OEM](https://business.karafun.com/oem) describes partner API, catalog, synchronization and entitlement integration, subject to a partnership. Its [business terms](https://business.karafun.com/terms) restrict downloads and resale without authorization. Confirm desktop platform support and commercial model.
- [Sunfly](https://api.sunflykaraoke.com/) describes protected delivery, partner SDKs including Electron by request, explicit metadata by agreement, and usage reporting. Verify exact-version metadata, playback SDK access, territory and license terms. A provider flag is not a verified clean rating when absent.
- [Stingray](https://karaoke-api-doc.stingray.com/docs/introduction/design-guidelines/) publishes integration guidance restricting offline storage and exposed song URLs; its [general information](https://karaoke-api-doc.stingray.com/docs/introduction/general-information/) describes play reporting. Obtain partner authorization and confirm permitted use and platforms.
- [Party Tyme](https://www.partytyme.com/) advertises a professional streaming product. Public product material does not establish authorization for this application to integrate or play its catalog; confirm partnership, terms and supported platforms.
- [LyricFind LyricIQ](https://www.lyricfind.com/products/lyriciq) describes lyric content filtering and classification. Confirm availability, category coverage, version matching, cost and permitted use of returned analysis. [LyricFind's description of its Tuned Global partnership](https://emu-glockenspiel-tn75.squarespace.com/press/jan23-tuned-global-and-lyricfind-announce-partnership) reports age-appropriateness filtering.
- Musixmatch is a candidate for licensed lyrics and metadata. The public material reviewed did not establish rights to obtain lyrics for independent automated classification, matching accuracy for karaoke versions, or API pricing; request terms before choosing it.

Store any future advisory or separately labeled automated assessment on the exact recording/version with source, confidence, coverage, time and provenance. An MP4 or CDG display is not proof of accessible, licensed lyric text. Host decisions, provider advisories and automated assessments must remain distinguishable.
