# 0.10.12

- Mobile opens as a singer companion with Library, Room, Playlists, and Profile. The standalone mobile host workflow is paused.
- Singers can browse all searchable pages of the Karaoke Nerds snapshot, turn on public KaraFun and Party Tyme Pro search, and save recordings to on-device playlists before joining.
- Join a nearby desktop room or enter its room code or link. Joined search uses the host’s enabled queueable sources, including connected KaraFun and Party Tyme accounts. Provider sign-in and playback stay on the desktop.
- Leaving a room keeps its supported public browsing sources as the next default. Public Party Tyme Pro and the host’s Party Tyme subscription list are separate editions.
