# YouTube acquisition and local playback

Search directly in **Song Library** to see local songs and Karaoke Nerds recordings together. Local matches appear immediately, and catalog results follow after a short typing pause. Double-click a recording, press Enter on its row, or right-click **Add to Queue**; choose an existing singer or enter a new name. Catalog recordings are added and requested through the host authority in one step, and submitting the request schedules their permanent yt-dlp download. The host sees progress, cancellation and failures. A metadata entry or queue entry alone is not a saved video.

Exact YouTube IDs are merged so an existing library song is reused with its host-edited metadata. Separate recordings of the same song remain separate choices. The **YouTube** badge identifies the service in place of a local DJ/track code. Content policy and library filters apply to catalog matches too; new recordings are unrated and stay hidden under clean-only or explicit include-list policies. Offline catalog results show their saved time and may be outdated. A catalog connection failure leaves local matches available.

The separate **Karaoke Nerds** page still accepts a YouTube link, title and optional artist and offers manual catalog additions and previews. Its **Add to Library** action saves metadata without starting a download and is optional for the ordinary search-and-queue workflow. A saved song's right-click **Download Video / Retry** action remains available if a download failed or its media is missing.

Choose the base folder in **Preferences → Video Downloads & Karaoke Nerds Cache**. The existing `karaokeNerdsCacheDirectory` JSON key is retained for compatibility. Blank uses **The Proper Volume Karaoke** within the operating system's Videos folder. `ytDlpExecutable` may specify an absolute executable path; blank discovers yt-dlp on PATH. FFmpeg must also be on PATH. The tools are not bundled. Both are installed and were tested on the current development machine.

Mapped Karaoke Nerds brands use `BASE/karaokenerds/Brands/BRAND_SLUG/ARTIST/ARTIST - TITLE.mp4`. Ordinary links or unmapped results use `BASE/youtube/ARTIST/ARTIST - TITLE.mp4`. Slugs come from saved brand mappings, not guessed brand codes or display names. Filenames are made safe for the host filesystem; a video-ID suffix resolves an occupied filename without overwriting another recording. The adjacent `.tpv.json` sidecar records ownership and the full fingerprint for verified reuse. See [download engine](youtube-downloads.md).

Library recordings show **Saved locally**, **Download needed**, or **Downloads when queued** alongside their **YouTube** service badge. After saving, playback uses the ordinary local MP4 player, selected audio output, global volume, timeline, rotation and history, including offline. It does not open an embedded player for queued performances. Show in Files and original-file relinking are available for saved assets. Downloading is separate from playback: completion never starts a song. Press Play when ready. A recording whose download has not finished cannot begin its performance.

The completed file stays on the host drive after Stop, restart or app exit. Reopening reuses the media already attached to the same song; retrying an unattached exact-video download checks the ownership sidecar and hash before reusing it. Missing saved media needs explicit Download Video / Retry or relinking; changed bytes fail verification. Stop the performance before replacing an attached active video's file. The app never silently substitutes another recording. A download failure does not consume a singer's turn. Keep videos and sidecars in media backups; show-database backups do not include them.

A separate **Preview YouTube** command still opens the official embedded player with native controls. This preview uses system audio, needs internet, closes on blur/minimize/hide, and cannot overlap karaoke or bumper playback. It does not save a video. It is separate from the cached show path. Paid providers, YouTube sign-in and general YouTube search remain unavailable. Brand/artist automatic batch selection remains planned; those saved preferences do not start jobs.

Validation: `node scripts/youtube-cache-smoke.mjs` after building uses an isolated profile, muted synthetic MP4, a fake yt-dlp worker and blocked network. It checks progress, permanent publication, unchanged version/request identity, no autoplay, local playback and seek, then restart/offline reuse without a second worker call. `node scripts/unified-library-smoke.mjs` adds a synthetic shared catalog and exercises ordinary library search, exact-ID merging, late replies, the singer dialog via double-click and Enter, queue-time download, local matches during outages and saved offline results. It uses no provider credentials or real media downloads. `node scripts/streaming-smoke.mjs` delegates to the cache check for compatibility. The real engine also downloaded the owner's Nada Surf – Amateur video and verified H.264/AAC and subsequent zero-network reuse. Physical mixer routing, real-phone behavior, all producer files and platforms still require verification.
